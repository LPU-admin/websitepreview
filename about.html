About

A two-person partnership serving museums and informal learning organizations.

The partnership

Launch Pad Unlimited is a general partnership working across Southern California. We work with science centers, zoos, aquariums, libraries, makerspaces, and the people who run them.

Launch Pad Unlimited helps museums and informal learning organizations build exhibits, adopt AI, train staff, and measure impact. What makes us different is what happens after the project. Every exhibit comes with documentation your team can use, and every training program lives in your organization, not with the person who built it. Every tool is integrated into the systems you already use, whether that's Google Drive, an internal wiki, or your LMS. Your team can run, maintain, and grow the work on their own, and we're available for check-ins, updates, and support whenever you want us.

Between the two of us, the work splits roughly into two halves: the learning, systems, and evaluation side, and the physical, hands-on, and community side. Most projects draw on both.

Jose Carrillo

Partner

Today he runs technology operations and leads AI adoption at USC's Iovine and Young Academy. He holds an M.Ed. in Learning Design and Technology from USC.
