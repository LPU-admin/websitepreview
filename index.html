Services

Eight ways we can help

Each one stands on its own. Most engagements combine two or three.

AI Integration & Enablement

A considered answer to where AI tools belong in your organization, and hands-on help putting them to work.

Exhibit Design & Fabrication

Exhibits built to be run by your team, handed over with documentation they can actually use.

Makerspace Planning & Buildout

From an empty room to a working space with the right tools, layout, safety practices, and programming.

Learning Design & Curriculum

Programs, workshops, and educator resources built for how people learn in informal settings.

Community Programming

Events, series, and partnerships that bring the surrounding community in and keep them coming back.

IT & Systems

The infrastructure behind programs and operations, for organizations without a full IT department.

Evaluation & Data

Finding out whether programs are working, and being able to show it to a board, funder, or council.

Staffing Support

More hands for program delivery, events, and coverage during expansions and busy seasons.
